## A. Science channel (Strange If True, "Wild ideas. Honest physics."): "What if you fell through the Earth?"
**Hook (0-2 s):** "You jump into a hole. Here is the ride, in numbers." Vertical, about 40 s, one continuous drop, ending on a loop-friendly line.
**Voice:** ElevenLabs (paid plan, commercial licence). Script: OpenAI gpt-5.4-mini; Claude Director; a claim-check pass on every number.
**Facts to verify before writing (do not trust memory):** crust thickness, mantle and core temperatures and pressures, fall time (about 38 min through a uniform-density Earth, longer in reality), speed at the centre. Label speculation clearly.

| Beat | Seconds | Picture and motion (Wan, from its own keyframe) | On-screen number |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0-6 | Edge of a circular shaft in rock, first-person drop begins, dust streams upward | start |
| 2 | 6-12 | Crust: layered strata rushing past, heat shimmer | depth, temperature |
| 3 | 12-20 | Mantle: glowing orange rock walls, slow churning | temperature, pressure |
| 4 | 20-28 | Outer core: liquid metal glow, camera still falling | temperature |
| 5 | 28-34 | Inner core: white-hot metal sphere, weightless moment at the centre | speed peak |
| 6 | 34-40 | Rising out the other side, light returns, loop back to beat 1 framing | end |

**Camera:** real forward fall (prompted as movement down the shaft), plus my shot-level edit (2-4 s shots, whip on layer changes). This avoids the static-camera problem seen in the film.
**Look gate before any long render:** one 5 s test beat first; owner judges motion by eye.

## B. Dreamscapes channel (Liminal Drift): "The hallway that keeps adding doors"
**Hook:** an endless corridor, a door at the end that is always a different door. 15 s, music-led, no narration, soft grain, hard cuts.
**Series device (audience steers the dream):** last frame shows two doors, a pale blue one and a warm yellow one. Pinned comment: "A or B?". The winner is the next Short's first room.
**Shots (4, one world: pastel carpet, wallpaper, soft light, one recurring "hero" description in every picture, one seed per Short):**
1. Standing view down a long carpeted hallway, slow walking pace forward, doors on both sides.
2. A door ahead opens by itself onto a bright child's bedroom, slow push through.
3. The bedroom's far wall is another hallway; walk on, scale feels slightly wrong.
4. Two doors, blue and yellow, camera stops, gentle sway, hold.
**Rules from earlier tests (dream-shorts-lessons):** no phone/person/hand/holding words in any Wan prompt; camera like a person walking, never a drone glide; no announcing effects (haze, heavy vignette).

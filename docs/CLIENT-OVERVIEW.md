## What this is
A private AI video studio that turns an idea (a topic, a script or a short brief) into a finished video: researched script, narration, music and sound, moving visuals, edit, captions and a ready-to-publish kit (title, description, thumbnail, tags). It makes long films (up to about 15 minutes) and vertical Shorts.

## What you get
- **A finished video file**, edited and mixed, with captions.
- **Shorts cut-downs** in vertical format when requested.
- **A publishing kit:** title options, description, tags, thumbnail, and an AI-disclosure setting for YouTube.
- **An editable timeline** (XML that opens in DaVinci Resolve or Premiere) if you want to adjust the cut yourself.
- **A short quality report** for each film: loudness, motion checks and any shots that needed repair.

## How it works with you
1. **Brief.** You send the topic, audience, length, tone and any must-have facts or visuals. **[OWNER TO FILL: intake form or channel]**
2. **Plan.** We return a written plan: scenes, narration script, characters and locations, visual style. You approve or change it before any expensive rendering.
3. **Preview.** Selected shots and a rough cut are shared for review. You can approve, reject or ask for a specific fix on any shot, for example "keep the framing, make the wing less blue".
4. **Final render.** Approved scenes are generated, upscaled, mixed and checked automatically.
5. **Delivery.** You receive the files and the publishing kit. Publishing itself stays your decision.

Every step can run fully automatic, with a review window, or fully manual, depending on how much control you want.

## What makes the result good
- Real camera movement in shots (push-ins, pans), not still images panned across the screen.
- The same character and place look the same from shot to shot (a character and location bank).
- Automatic checks on every cut: loudness (about -14 LUFS), black or frozen frames, caption timing, and near-static shots. A failed check holds the film for a human decision.
- Targeted repair: a bad moment inside a shot can be fixed without re-making the whole scene.

## Honest limits
- **Resolution:** shots are generated at about 720p and upscaled for 4K delivery. The final file is 4K, but the source detail is upscaled, not native 4K.
- **AI visuals can have flaws** (a colour shift, a warped detail). The gate catches many, not all; review is part of the process.
- **Motion length:** each generated shot is a few seconds; films are built from many shots.
- **Factual content:** narration facts are checked against the plan's claims list, but a human should review anything sensitive.
- **Disclosure:** realistic AI-made video should carry YouTube's AI-disclosure label. We set it in the publishing kit.

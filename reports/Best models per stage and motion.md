# Fix the plan before buying models

The ant film did not fail because Wan 2.2 is the wrong model. It failed because the pipeline asked it for the wrong thing in the configuration known to move least. Eighteen near-identical dark keyframes with no event written into them, a new camera preset every 5 s, the 4-step lightx2v distill on both experts, and vertical 720x1280 framing are together the setup the community reports as nearly static. The lightx2v maintainers have said in writing that the 4-step model moves more slowly than the full model. For model choice per stage, the evidence points to keeping most of what the owner already pays for: ElevenLabs v4 Turbo for narration (already the code default, and #1 on the Nov 2026 speech arena), Lyria 3.5 for music ($0.08 per song), gpt-5.4-mini for scripts until a new by-ear A/B says otherwise, and Gemini for clip review. The upgrades that matter are elsewhere: a sound-design stage built from ElevenLabs SFX plus a CC0 library; a top model (Claude Opus 5.5) for planning and shot design, which costs under ₹10 per video; a keyframe model ranked above FLUX.2 klein (931 Elo, now about 230 points below cheap hosted options); and a sampler and camera recipe that restores motion before any hosted video spend. Hosted image-to-video has moved on. MAGI-2 ranks #1 for image-to-video at $0.08 per second (₹7.5/s), which makes a hero-shot hybrid practical for documentaries, though not inside a ₹20 Short. Every price below was read on 25 Nov 2026 at about ₹94 per dollar, and several change within weeks.

## The ant film broke in planning, then in the sampler

Successful AI video productions settle the content before paying for motion. One finished, ideal still per cut is then animated, which a 2026 production-training guide calls more reliable than prompting video directly ([EasyEase](https://note.com/easyease_/n/nedad1b9a0cb2?hl=en)). Kling's own guides add an animatic: storyboard panels in order, with rough durations and scratch audio, used to test pacing before generation ([Kling animatics](https://kling.ai/blog/animatics)). The ant film skipped the step that matters: its 18 keyframes were variations of one meadow, so no camera move could make anything happen. The prompt guides agree on the cause at clip level. Wan 2.2 weights the start of the prompt most, and when a clip has no motion cue it comes out static or morphing; a slow camera makes a clip feel slow even when the subject moves ([wan27.org prompt guide](https://www.wan27.org/blog/wan-2-2-prompt-guide); [veed.io](https://www.veed.io/learn/wan-2-2-prompting-guide)). Each shot therefore needs one concrete event with a beginning and an end, written subject first, then action verb, then camera. Strong verbs ("burst", "snap", "rush") and one action per clip are the reported pattern ([Apatero](https://apatero.com/blog/wan-2-2-faster-motion-prompting-techniques-2025)).

The sampler then made a weak brief worse. On 13 Aug 2025 the lightx2v maintainers acknowledged that the 4-step Wan 2.2 Lightning model's motion "tends to be slower" than the base model. Community testers found that a "3-sampler" fix rescued horizontal resolutions, but **vertical resolutions stayed nearly static** ([HF discussion #26](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Wan2.2-Lightning/discussions/26)). That matches the studio's 720x1280 output exactly. The fixes with community evidence are a few undistilled high-noise steps at real CFG first, CFG of about 2–3.5 on the high-noise expert only, distill LoRA strength 0.6–0.8 on high-noise with 1.0 on low-noise, or 8 total steps split 4+4. Reports conflict: one user found CFG alone did nothing while a lower LoRA strength helped ([HF discussion #20](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Wan2.2-Lightning/discussions/20); [HF discussion #5](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Wan2.2-Lightning/discussions/5)). lightx2v also shipped full 720p I2V 4-step distilled checkpoints on 12 Apr 2026, trained with an "optimized low-noise training algorithm", in about 15 GB FP8-ComfyUI form. The model card makes no claim about motion speed ([lightx2v/Wan2.2-Distill-Models](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Wan2.2-Distill-Models)). Community "slow-mo fix" LoRAs exist but have not been independently evaluated ([rzgar Slow-Mo Fix](https://huggingface.co/rzgar/Wan2.2_I2V_LightX2V_Slow-Mo_Fix)). Distillation also weakens camera adherence, which the same maintainer noted in September 2025 ([HF discussion #26](https://huggingface.co/lightx2v/Wan2.2-Lightning/discussions/26)).

The jerky camera is a planning defect, not a model defect. Fun Camera presets start every clip from zero velocity, so picking a new preset every 5 s beat guarantees a visible change of direction at each join. Two fixes follow from the sources. The first is continuity conditioning: SVI 2.0 Pro for Wan 2.2 conditions each 81-frame clip on a reference anchor plus the **last 4–12 frames' motion latents**, not a single last frame, so velocity carries across the seam. A community run reports 20 s at 1280x720 with "no visible transitions" in about 340 s ([neura.market mirror](https://neura.market/directories/stable-diffusion/posts/reddit-1q1jmz7)). A production test passed 9 of 14 cases (64%) and found that boundary discontinuities "drop sharply" ([Atlas Cloud](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/guides/long-video-generation-blog-2)). The second is an authored path: one camera move per scene, sliced per clip. Uni3C turns a monocular-depth point cloud from the keyframe into camera guidance, ships in core ComfyUI as `WanUni3CControlnetApply`, and can copy a camera move from a reference clip ([Comfy docs](https://docs.comfy.org/built-in-nodes/WanUni3CControlnetApply); [RunComfy workflow](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/uni3c-comfyui-workflow-video-referenced-camera-motion-transfer)). Its paper claims it beats CamI2V, ViewCrafter and SEVA on camera accuracy, but no source compares it with Fun Camera or VACE, and Wan 2.2 A14B compatibility is unconfirmed ([arXiv 2504.14899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14899)). Prompt-only camera words are unreliable; one hands-on test found an orbit instruction "totally ignored" ([aistudynow](https://aistudynow.com/how-to-write-better-wan-2-2-prompts-tested-in-comfyui/)).

For events the viewer must see, such as an object travelling or a creature crossing frame, trajectory control is the open tool. ATI (ByteDance, Wan 2.1 I2V-14B, with a native ComfyUI template) handles object, local and camera motion from drawn paths ([Comfy docs: ATI](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/video/wan/wan-ati)). Wan-Move (Alibaba, NeurIPS 2025, reportedly Apache 2.0) propagates first-frame features along dense tracks at 832x480. Its authors say its controllability rivals Kling's Motion Brush, an author claim ([arXiv 2512.08765](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08765v1); [Comfy docs](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/video/wan/wan-move.md)). Both are 480p-class and need upscaling. The licences for ATI, ReCamMaster and Uni3C weights were not checked and may be research-only.

Frame rate comes last, because interpolation does not add motion; it only makes a near-static clip smoother. Standard practice is to interpolate Wan's 16 fps by 2x or 3x and retime to 24 ([Comfy docs: frame interpolation](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/utility/frame-interpolation)). GIMM-VFI holds fast pans where RIFE ghosts, but takes about 4 minutes against about 50 s for an 81-frame 2x job. On slow footage the two are hard to tell apart ([comfy.icu GIMM](https://comfy.icu/node/GIMMVFI_interpolate)). Interpolating to 48 fps and frame-blending down to 24 to simulate shutter blur is a common editing trick, but nothing in the sources verifies it for AI video, so it needs an A/B by eye. Native 50 fps exists in LTX-2.x ([NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-sg/geforce/news/rtx-ai-video-generation-guide/)), but LTX ranks 832–914 Elo on image-to-video against 1163 for the leader ([AA I2V](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/image-to-video)). Reviewers rate Wan 2.2 higher on motion realism ([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/ltx-2-3-vs-wan-2-2-comparison-2026)).

## Hosted video now leads on motion, and MAGI-2 makes hero shots affordable

The open Wan line still ends at Wan 2.2 (Apache 2.0). Wan 2.5 to 3.0 had no published weights as of 5 Nov 2026 ([kenerateai](https://kenerateai.com/blogs/is-wan-3-0-open-source)). The hosted leaderboard has turned over completely: Sora 2's API was scheduled to end on 24 Sep 2026 ([DEV write-up](https://dev.to/mr_manushukla/sora-2-and-the-videos-api-shut-down-on-24-september-2026-the-migration-and-the-real-cost-per-second-bek)), and Veo 3.1 left the Gemini API on 22 Oct 2026 ([DEV comparison](https://dev.to/alex_martllm/seedance-kling-and-veo-31-api-prices-per-second-compared-across-6-sellers-november-2026-287o)). Artificial Analysis's blind-vote image-to-video board is the relevant one for a keyframe-first studio, and on it value and rank do not track each other ([AA I2V](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/image-to-video)):

Resellers matter: fal and Replicate charge about twice the official rate for Seedance and Kling ([DEV comparison](https://dev.to/alex_martllm/seedance-kling-and-veo-31-api-prices-per-second-compared-across-6-sellers-november-2026-287o)). That source's author runs one of the sellers compared, so verify live per rule 7. The ToS on commercial output for MAGI-2, Seedance, Wan 3.0 and Omni Flash were not checked and must be read before a hero shot is published.

The arithmetic decides where hosted video belongs. At the owner's measured $0.89/h for a secure 4090, a 5 s Wan clip that takes 3 GPU-minutes costs about $0.045, or **₹0.84 per video second**, roughly 9x cheaper than MAGI-2. Hosted video only becomes cheaper than Wan above about 54 GPU-minutes per 10 s of video, which no realistic workflow reaches. **So the hybrid decision is about quality, not cost.** For a 10-minute documentary, putting 10% hero shots on MAGI-2 adds about ₹450. For a 60 s Short, one 5 s MAGI-2 hero shot alone costs about ₹38. Even an all-Wan Short at 3 GPU-minutes per clip costs about ₹50, already above the ₹20 target. That target needs re-checking against measured warm-pod times, not these assumed timings.

MiniMax H3 is the tempting open alternative: 33B parameters, native 32 kHz audio, 4–15 s at 24 fps, and #3 on text-to-video. Its community licence, however, is described as "source-available, with territory restrictions" that appear to exclude the US, EU, UK and South Korea ([RunPod blog](https://www.runpod.io/blog/minimax-h3-the-open-weight-omni-modal-video-model-and-what-it-takes-to-run-it)). It needs an 80 GB GPU for a 42.5 GB pruned checkpoint ([Spheron](https://www.spheron.network/blog/deploy-minimax-h3-gpu-cloud/)) and a 600 GB download. Running it in a US or EU RunPod data centre may itself breach the licence, so self-hosting is **HIGH** risk. The hosted H3 at $0.08/s avoids the question. HunyuanVideo 1.5 carries a similar EU/UK/South Korea exclusion ([Thunder Compute](https://www.thundercompute.com/blog/best-open-source-ai-video-generation-models)).

On physics-heavy events (water, fabric, impacts), 2026 roundups rate Kling and Seedance above open models, but they are vendor and affiliate pages with no benchmark behind them ([atlabs](https://www.atlabs.ai/blog/5-best-ai-video-models-for-motion-control-(2026-comparison)); [runware](https://runware.ai/blog/ai-video-model-comparison)). No reliable source documents a "hero hosted, rest open" workflow among creators. Vendors do describe choosing a model per shot by need ([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/ltx-2-3-vs-wan-2-2-comparison-2026)). The owner's own by-eye A/B of Wan-with-fixes against MAGI-2 on three ant-film shots is the evidence that would settle it, at a cost of about ₹115 in hosted credit.

## Keyframes, finishing and the text stages are cheap to upgrade

Keyframes are where the visible picture quality is set, and the studio's model has fallen behind. On AA's text-to-image board, **FLUX.2 [klein] 9B sits at 931 Elo (#85)**. MAI-Image-2.7-Flash scores 1160 at $0.010 per image (₹0.94) and Nano Banana 2.1 scores 1175 at $0.034 (₹3.2). GPT Image 2.6 leads at 1264 but costs $0.21 ([AA T2I](https://artificialanalysis.ai/image/leaderboard/text-to-image)). Among open weights, the best options are MiniMax H3-Image (1066), Qwen-Image-2.1 (1049) and FLUX 3 Image Klein (1040), whose licences were not verified ([AA open-weights T2I](https://artificialanalysis.ai/image/leaderboard/text-to-image/open-weights)). At 12–18 keyframes and 3–4 candidates each, MAI Flash costs about ₹35–70 per film and Nano Banana about ₹115–230. Both are small next to clip cost, and both are edit-capable families, which helps keep a character or place consistent across a storyboard. A text-to-image Elo is not a test of how well a still animates, so the switch should be judged on animated results.

For finishing, FlashVSR (Apache 2.0) took a 10 s 720p clip to 4K in about 52 s on an RTX 5090, against about 312 s for SeedVR2 to 1080p. SeedVR2 can over-sharpen clean AI footage, and Topaz Astra is the paid option for cinematic polish ([ComfyUI upscaling handbook](https://blog.comfy.org/p/upscaling-in-comfyui)). FlashVSR fits the 4K target as a single pass over the cut.

Text-model costs are negligible next to GPU time, so the quality-first rule costs almost nothing here. Claude Opus 5.5 costs $4/$20 per million tokens and Sonnet 5.5 costs $2/$10 (Claude API reference, cached 3 Nov 2026; live source [anthropic.com/pricing](https://www.anthropic.com/pricing)). The OpenAI ladder runs from gpt-5.4-mini at $0.75/$4.50 to gpt-6.2-sol at $2/$10 and gpt-5.6-sol at $4/$20, a promotional price that ends 30 Nov ([OpenAI pricing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing)). A full video's plan, research, script, shots and prompts come to roughly 50–100K tokens, or ₹47–141 even on Opus-class models. The creative-writing leaderboards disagree: Claude Opus 5 is #1 on EQ-Bench but #10 on the human-voted arena, and Kimi K3 is #2 against #23. EQ-Bench uses Claude judges, and newer models are not always better there ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/which-ai-model-writes-best-leaderboards-disagree)). That disagreement is why the owner's 23 Nov by-ear pick of gpt-5.4-mini for scripts should stand until a new A/B against Opus 5.5 and gpt-5.6-sol. The planner and shot designer are a different case. The ant film's failure, 18 interchangeable shots with no events, is a creative-direction failure, and there a top model at high effort costs pennies.

For research, Gemini Search grounding gives 5,000 free requests a month, then $14 per 1K ([Google AI Studio pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing)). Perplexity's Search API costs $5 per 1K ([Perplexity pricing](https://docs.perplexity.ai/getting-started/pricing)). Claude's `web_search_20260209` plus `web_fetch` reads cited pages in full. Fact-check quality depends on reading the page, not the snippet.

For review, Gemini is the only first-party API that takes real video with audio. It costs about 300 tokens per second at high resolution plus 32 for audio, at a sampling rate settable up to 24 fps ([Gemini video docs](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/video-understanding)). That makes a 5 s clip about ₹0.23 on gemini-4-hydrogen. Price is not the constraint; reliability is. Artifact-Bench tested 19 multimodal LLMs and found many near or below chance on hard realism tasks, with "significant misalignment" with human preferences ([arXiv 2605.18984](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18984)). The workable design is to sample at 4–8 fps rather than the 1 fps default, which misses flicker and jitter. The judge should answer pairwise questions ("which take shows more visible action?") and the content question it is good at ("does a visible event happen?"), not give an absolute realism score. Calibrate it against about 20 owner ratings before it runs unattended in Auto. Watch prices: Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon double on 1 Dec 2026, and Gemini 3.8 Flash doubles on 1 Jan 2027.

## Sound design is the missing stage, and it is mostly mixing

The narration model is not what is wrong with the narration. The worker already defaults to `eleven_v4_turbo`, which tops the 25 Nov Speech Arena at 1342 Elo, just ahead of Eleven v4 at 1339. Eleven v3 has fallen to 1194 ([AA TTS leaderboard](https://artificialanalysis.ai/text-to-speech/leaderboard)). Turbo costs $0.04 per 1K characters and v4 costs $0.08, roughly ₹3.8 and ₹7.5 per finished minute ([ElevenLabs API pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api)). Starter includes a commercial licence and about 30,000 credits a month, though the API page lists "75,000 included TTS (v4) characters", a conflict to check on the account's usage page ([ElevenLabs pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing)). The owner's open dissatisfaction with the voice is therefore about voice choice, pacing and delivery, so the next test is v4 against v4 Turbo on slow documentary reading and a different voice, not a new vendor. Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS (1284 Elo, about ₹1.3 per minute until 31 Dec) is a strong cheap fallback. Fish Audio Drama 3 (1275) is the only open model near the top. Chatterbox (1023) sits far below, which confirms its retirement.

Music stays with Lyria. `lyria-3.5` costs $0.08 per full song in the Gemini API ([Google AI Studio pricing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing)). Its commercial terms fall under the general Gemini API terms rather than a stated licence ([therundown](https://www.therundown.ai/tools/lyria-3-5)), which is an owner decision before monetisation. ElevenLabs Music ($0.15/min, commercial use from Starter) is the explicitly licensed second source. Suno gives paid users a commercial licence but "generally" not ownership, has no official API, and faces ongoing litigation, so it is a poor fit for an automated pipeline ([Digital Music News](https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2025/12/22/suno-warner-music-deal-changes/)).

Sound effects are where licences bite. ElevenLabs Sound Effects costs $0.12/min through the API, or 200 credits a generation on the plan, and is commercially clean ([ElevenLabs API pricing](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing/api)). The strong open video-to-audio models are not clean for commercial use. MMAudio's checkpoints are CC-BY-NC 4.0 ([MMAudio GitHub](https://github.com/hkchengrex/MMAudio)). HunyuanVideo-Foley, which scored highest in an independent comparison (0.51 against MMAudio's 0.39), uses Tencent's community licence and blocks the EU ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/tencent/HunyuanVideo-Foley/blob/main/README.md); [ControlFoley](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15086)). ThinkSound's cards contradict each other. Pixabay sounds are free for commercial use without attribution ([Pixabay FAQ](https://pixabay.com/service/faq/)). Freesound CC0 files are equally safe, but CC-BY-NC files must be filtered out. The free BBC archive is non-commercial only ([CDM](https://cdm.link/bbc-gives-away-16k-wav-sound-effects-disallows-using/)).

The mixing practice is consistent across practitioners. Narration is the anchor; music and ambience duck under it; effects land on actions, cuts and reveals; and "cartoony" sounds draw attention to themselves ([Overseer OS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/youtube-sound-design); [Descript](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-to-sound-design-a-youtube-video)). Master to about -14 LUFS integrated with true peak at or below -1 dBTP, because YouTube turns louder uploads down ([HackerNoon](https://hackernoon.com/how-to-master-ai-generated-film-audio-for-youtube); [Production Advice](https://productionadvice.co.uk/youtube-loudness/)). A counterpoint holds that loudness should result from a good mix, not be chased as a goal ([Sound On Sound](https://www.soundonsound.com/techniques/ian-shepherd-loudness-dynamics)). Evidence that music or risers lift retention is weak: a 2026 CHI paper found background music's effect on recall negligible, though it had a medium effect on persuasion ([City St George's](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/37604/1/3772318.3791466.pdf)). The ant film's silent gaps are a script-timing defect. A gate should require the narration to cover every beat, or the plan to mark the gap as a deliberate sound moment with ambience and a cue.

Captions should be forced-aligned to the known script rather than transcribed. A 2026 benchmark measured a mean word-boundary error of 96.8 ms for stable-ts against 19.1 ms for Montreal Forced Aligner ([arXiv 2610.17782](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.17782)). WhisperX's wav2vec2 alignment pass runs locally on the laptop ([WhisperX](https://github.com/m-bain/whisperx)).

## Creators budget takes, gate spend, and cut faster than five seconds

The documented productions plan for waste and spend it deliberately. invideo's 3-minute Seedance episode generated 164 clips to keep 41, about 25%. More than 40% of the kept shots were stitched from two or more takes, and it cost about $315 per finished minute ([invideo FAQ](https://invideo.io/faq/how-many-ai-video-generations-does-it-take-to-produce-a/)). A Veo commercial planned "roughly 25 takes per kept shot" ([ScreenWeaver](https://www.screenweaver.ai/blog/veo-vs-kling-commercials)). Model makers now ship draft-then-final paths: LTX drafts at about 280p and upscales only once motion and pacing look right ([LTX docs](https://docs.ltx.io/open-source-model/usage-guides/two-stage-generation)), and a Seedance 2.5 draft is a 480p preview whose 1080p final reuses the task ID ([ComfyUI docs](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/partner-nodes/bytedance/seedance-2-5-draft.md)). A Wan 2.2 community workflow drafts on the high-noise expert at low resolution and renders the final at target resolution with the low-noise expert, a reported 32–49% time saving ([PromptHero](https://prompthero.com/ai-models/wan-2-2-i2v-hd-fhd-resolution-but-much-faster-download)). Human gates sit before spend: n8n builders found that reviewing the finished draft is "too late" to save credits, and their templates put approval on the script, beats and storyboard, with per-scene regeneration that keeps the narration ([n8n community](https://community.n8n.io/t/generate-faceless-youtube-shorts-with-n8n-script-voice-and-captions/326966); [n8n workflow 21952](https://n8n.io/workflows/21952-create-narrated-faceless-youtube-shorts-with-openai-flux-kling-and-elevenlabs/)). Cinestack's hold-before-paid design already matches this. What it lacks is the cheap preview tier between keyframe and final render.

Pacing benchmarks come from vendors, but they agree in direction. High-performing Shorts cut every 2–4 s, storytelling needs a new visual or story beat every 5–7 s, the first 3 s account for most drop-off, and burned-in captions help ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-youtube-shorts-length-format-retention)). Hooks pair a visual change in the first second with a 5–9-word overlay restating the spoken hook, because Shorts often start muted ([vidIQ hooks](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/viral-video-hooks-youtube-shorts/)). A 90 s film with one slow glide per 5 s and no event fails every one of these tests. YouTube policy adds a structural constraint. Since 15 Jul 2025, "inauthentic" content explicitly includes image slideshows and templated storylines with little narrative value ([YouTube Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)). From 16 Jul 2026, generic, repetitive or template content is non-monetisable and judged at channel level ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/20/youtube-clarifies-policies-around-ai-slop-and-upsetting-videos/)). Realistic synthetic footage must be disclosed, which does not limit reach or monetisation ([YouTube Help 14328491](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491)). Near-static glides over similar frames sit close to the slideshow line, so the motion problem is a monetisation risk as well as a taste problem.

## Conclusion

The research changes where the studio should spend effort. Model shopping looked like the lever, but the stages that failed (planning, camera continuity, sound) are the cheapest to fix. The stage that looked expensive, hosted video, turns out to be a quality option priced at ₹7.5 per second rather than a cost problem. Text models cost a rounding error, so the quality-first rule says to use the best planner available. Open video stays the workhorse because it is about 9x cheaper per second, Apache-licensed and controllable, provided the sampler is configured for motion rather than speed. The one place where "best model" and "use it in Auto" separate is judgement. Both multimodal judges and open foley models are weakest exactly where the owner's eye and ear are strongest, so they belong in triage and Manual mode until calibrated.

The largest unknowns are measurable within a day. Nobody has published how much the motion fixes recover at vertical 720p, whether Uni3C works on Wan 2.2 A14B, or how MAGI-2 compares on this studio's own subjects. Three small A/Bs (sampler, keyframe model, hero shot) costing under ₹400 together would replace most of this report's inference with the owner's own evidence. Several prices move within five weeks: the gpt-5.6-sol promotion ends 30 Nov, Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon double on 1 Dec, the ElevenLabs Starter offer ends 1 Dec, and Gemini TTS and Gemini 3.8 Flash double on 1 Jan 2027. Re-verify each price before wiring it into `config/providers.yaml`.
